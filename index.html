I am a third-year PhD candidate of the School of Computer Science at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China.
I broadly pursue research in Applied Cryptography, Post-Quantum Cryptography, 5G Network Security, and AI Security and Privacy. In addition, I am interested in Quantum Key Distribution. My research aims to:

"Building an open and safe Internet accssible to everyone, where privacy and security are inviolable."


I hope to improve the privacy preserving and security of the Internet (especially 5G networks) and increasingly powerful AI by developing new primitives and protocols. I am transitioning my research focus from 5G Network Security to AI Security and Privacy, designing AI defense and user privacy preservation methods for increasingly powerful AI models. If you are also interested in it, please feel free to reach out to me for collaboration.
